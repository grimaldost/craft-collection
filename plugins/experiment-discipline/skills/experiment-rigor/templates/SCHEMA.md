## Tiers

The `tier` field selects the required-field set and which context gates apply: probe (cheap, refuses a confirmatory verdict or a posterior), measurement (a frozen pre-registration and a reported CI), and decision (adds the comprehension gate).

## Comprehension rubric (Q3, decision tier)

A decision-tier record carries a `comprehension` block: at least two fresh-context readers, each answering the four Methods-reconstruction questions verbatim with a resolving `transcript_path`. The block passes only on unanimous four-question reconstruction by every reader; genuineness of the reads is ceded to review.

| Question key | What the reader must recover |
|---|---|
| `manipulated` | What was manipulated -- the independent variable / intervention? |
| `placement` | Where was the intervention text placed in the prompt? |
| `operationalization` | How was each outcome operationalized -- the verifier and its check? |
| `execution_real` | Was execution real -- runs actually spawned and graded, not narrated? |

## Rounding and tolerance policy

Record CI bounds are stored rounded to 4 decimal places. The validator recomputes each interval with stats.py and asserts equality within ATOL = 1e-9 and RTOL = 1e-6 against those 4-decimal values. Integers and version strings cross-check exactly; `cost_usd_est` cross-checks within max(1%, $0.01). report.md is serialized under the canonical policy (sorted keys, LF, repr floats) so a re-render is byte-identical.

## Statistical prior

Prior: Beta(1, 1). Uniform within-experiment prior; the prior-sensitivity note lives in small-n-stats.md.

## Cross-experiment GRADE update (two-node worked example)

The chain carries a qualitative GRADE update, never pooled counts. Node A (a measurement experiment) establishes a posterior; node B links back with `updates.prior.source_id: ../node-a/record.yaml` and records a `certainty` from the four-level enum plus `downgrade_reasons[]`. `render.py --chain` walks the links into a root-first lineage view:

```
1. node-a (measurement)
2. node-b (decision) -- certainty=moderate, downgrades=[nondeterminism]
```
